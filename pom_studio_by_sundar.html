First-Principles Michel-Lévy & Banded Spherulite POM Studio

A free, interactive Michel-Lévy interference colour chart and polarised-light microscopy (POM) simulator. Interference colours are calculated from first principles, from spectral transmission and the CIE 1931 colour-matching functions, following Sørensen (2013), rather than painted by hand.

  • Calculated Michel-Lévy chart and Raith–Sørensen chart: read path difference (retardation), birefringence, thickness and interference order.
  • Specimen library of reference crystals and minerals: quartz, calcite, sapphire, magnesium fluoride, rutile, ice, fluorite, gypsum, olivine, muscovite and zircon.
  • Banded spherulite simulator with first-order red and quarter-wave compensator plates, optical sign and lamellar twist.
  • Colour-to-retardation matching from your own micrographs.
  • Export of PNG and SVG figures, colour tables, and Python or MATLAB scripts.

Designed, developed and maintained by Sundar Tanari. Public domain (CC0 1.0). Colour model: Sørensen, B.E. (2013), Eur. J. Mineral. 25, 5–10.
